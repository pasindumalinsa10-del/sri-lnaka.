An Island of Timeless Magic

Sri Lanka is a land of serendipity. From golden beaches kissed by turquoise waves to misty mountains carpeted in emerald tea plantations, this island packs an incredible variety of landscapes, wildlife, and ancient heritage into a compact tropical paradise.

Ancient Wonder

Sigiriya Rock

Known as the 'Lion Rock', this ancient fortress rises 200 meters above the jungle, featuring heavenly frescoes and water gardens.

Hill Country

Ella & Nine Arch

A mountain village surrounded by cloud forests and tea plantations, home to the iconic colonial-era Nine Arch Bridge.

Tropical Coast

Mirissa Beach

Famous for Coconut Tree Hill, vibrant nightlife, surfing waves, and being one of the best places in the world to spot Blue Whales.

Cultural Heart

Sacred Kandy

Set on a plateau surrounded by mountains, Kandy is home to the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic and vibrant cultural dances.

Wild Safari

Yala National Park

Experience untamed nature. Yala boasts the highest density of leopards in the world, alongside wild elephants and sloth bears.

Colonial Heritage

Galle Dutch Fort

Walk through cobble-stoned streets lined with Dutch-colonial buildings, boutique cafes, and an iconic seaside lighthouse.
